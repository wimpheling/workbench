## Description

Close the front-door follow-up at the design stage and transfer supplier and

## Discussion

The owner separated horizontal design work (major choices for each side) from
vertical validation (detailed product evidence, alignment, sag, sealing and
physical performance). The front is designed: two inset swing doors with no
centre post, the specified opening order, CFG hinges and spacers, corner
connections, 4 mm glass/FSP08 candidate and nominal perimeter/meeting seals.

The implementation and geometric evidence are recorded in
[front-door fit and gaskets](front-door-fit-and-gaskets.md),
[front glass and seal junctions](front-glass-and-seal-junctions.md), and
[the merged frame-header update](../in-progress/frame-header-layout.md).
The latter adapts the doors and seals to the upright front beam.
See [the current design](../../v3/docs/FRONT_DOORS.md).

Every former outstanding obligation is retained in the new validation task:
hinge fixings, spacers and stiffness; glass weight/sag and FSP08 approval;
seal products, compression and corner/end support; closure retention,
physical stops/hold-open details, fixing pitch and adjustment; squareness,
tolerances, travel/loading access, ordered cycling, forces and seal contact;
glass processing or wood moisture movement; and unresolved continuous-motion
bounds. None is marked physically validated by closing this follow-up.
