# Decide right-side air extraction and filtration

## Description

Determine whether the right side is an appropriate location for the air
outlet, whether active enclosure extraction is needed, and whether to use
HEPA filtration. The right side will not be reachable in the workshop.

## Discussion

The enclosure should contain wood particles while allowing deliberate air
circulation. Distinguish extraction at the CNC dust shoe from ventilation
of the enclosure itself. Outlet location, replacement air, filtration,
fan selection, and maintenance access need to be designed as one system.
HEPA filtration and active extraction are open options, not selected
equipment or established requirements.

An inaccessible right wall may constrain filter changes, cleaning, fan
servicing, and duct connections. Assess an alternative outlet location or
remotely accessible equipment before choosing a right-side assembly.
Coordinate with [front-door seals](front-door-fit-and-gaskets.md),
[bifold seals](../in-progress/bifold-door-mechanism.md),
[the roof and hose interface](roof-panels-and-gasket.md), and
[rear electrical mounting](back-electrical-mounting-and-cables.md).

## Implementation plan

- [ ] Establish the ventilation requirements
  - [ ] Document workshop clearances, available dust extraction, expected use, and possible discharge destinations
  - [ ] Compare passive ventilation and active enclosure extraction alongside dust-shoe collection
  - [ ] Assess filtration options, including HEPA, using manufacturer evidence and relevant guidance
- [ ] Agree on a complete, maintainable airflow arrangement
  - [ ] Compare right-side and alternative outlet positions with installation and service access
  - [ ] Define replacement-air entry, airflow direction, and measures to limit particle escape
  - [ ] If active extraction is selected, size flow and pressure allowance for ducts, inlet restrictions, and clean/loaded filters
  - [ ] Select real fan, filter, duct, and mounting references as applicable, including access for replacement and cleaning
- [ ] Implement and verify the agreed arrangement in v3
  - [ ] Add openings, equipment envelopes, duct routes, supports, seals, and maintenance clearances
  - [ ] Document calculated requirements separately from airflow and containment checks requiring measurement after assembly
  - [ ] Update supplier specifications, assembly instructions, and maintenance information
  - [ ] Run relevant tests, checks, and builds and record the results

## Open questions / blockers

- [ ] Is the inaccessible right wall suitable for an outlet, and can all service items be reached elsewhere?
- [ ] Will extracted air return to the workshop or discharge elsewhere?
- [ ] Which dust-shoe vacuum or collector is planned, and is separate enclosure extraction needed?
- [ ] Is HEPA filtration appropriate for the selected airflow and discharge arrangement?
- [ ] What flow, pressure, noise, power, and maintenance requirements should guide equipment selection?
- [ ] Where should replacement air enter without creating a direct particle escape path?
