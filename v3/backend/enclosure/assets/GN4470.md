These replace the earlier investigation's incorrect 50 × 7 × 10 mm body

positive Y. The earlier source-face interpretation was reversed; actual
counterbore geometry establishes this. Installed geometry rotates the body
180° about source Z to face counterbores away from the printed holder.
Source parts touch without volumetric penetration. `magnetic_catches.py`
exposes each intact centred solid, its source centre and bounding dimensions;
it neither scales geometry nor treats the source assembly as an installed catch.

The imported component geometry does not validate a parked mounting. The
proposed nominal 2 mm air gap is a design assumption; catalogue contact-force
data must not be applied at that gap. Mounting/approach clearance, printed
holder geometry, fixings, actual retention force and cycle life remain open.

## Rejected direct-mount screen

A Terra native-solid study placed the body at door-frame-local
`[-17.5,3,250]`, rotated +90° about source Y. Its rear face then sits on the
fixed jamb at Y=8. The L3 plate was attached to the primary leaf using source
rotations Y+90°, then Z+90°, and source origin `[-3.5,-15.27,250]` in leaf
coordinates. This leaves 1 mm to the leaf's end face and gives 2.002 mm
body/strike clearance at full park. It is **not an installed design**.

At opening fractions 0, .25, .5, .75, 1, native magnet/strike intersections
were 468.57, 431.59, 335.61, 27.65, 0 mm³. The strike also intersects the
fixed post at .5/.75 (21.18/48.05 mm³), and the perimeter seal when closed
(8.37 mm³). Its mounting-hole rows land at primary-local Y≈13.48/30.98,
not the slot datum Y=23. The mirrored rear has the same kinematic obstruction.

Reject this direct mounting, despite its favourable parked gap. A separate
offset-holder layout is required. No changes to the fitted assembly, catch
inventory or UI were justified by this rejected screen.

## Superseded offset-holder prototype

The user subsequently removed parked catches, holders and dedicated fixings
from the active design for simplicity. The downloaded STEP remains available
for reference; it is not active procurement or fitted geometry.

The subsequent [offset-holder integration](../../../docs/PARKED_CATCHES.md)
uses these unchanged solids with the corrected magnet orientation, printed
adapters and nominal metal M5 fixings. Five-pose native checks cover both
doors; the nominal parked gap is 2 mm. This is a modeled prototype, not a
retention-force or physical installation approval.
