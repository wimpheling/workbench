## Native datums and placement

Each file contains one intact solid with its bore along source X. The roller
extends X=0..7 mm, with 22 mm maximum diameter, 5 mm bore, recessed bearing
faces at X=1 and X=6, and nominal inner-race outer diameter 8.5 mm.
Its measured volume is 2124.567251 mm³. This is the vendor's combined roller
representation, not an exploded bearing or a simulated rolling contact.

The bush extends X=0..5 mm: an 8 mm diameter body at X=0..3, then a 5 mm
diameter centring collar at X=3..5, with a 4 mm through bore. Its measured
volume is 127.234502 mm³. X=0 is the outer clamping face; X=3 is the bearing
shoulder. The preceding app envelope incorrectly omitted the collar and
represented the body as only 5 mm diameter.

The loader recentres each native solid and rigidly rotates X onto model Z;
it never scales, cuts, rebuilds or replaces it with an annulus. The upper
bush reverses the axis, putting both collars inside the bearing bore.

With positive drop measured below the header underside, the roller remains
centred at 15 mm. Upper bush bounds are 9.5..14.5 mm, lower bush bounds
15.5..20.5 mm. Their shoulders contact the bearing faces at 12.5/17.5 mm;
collar tips are 1 mm apart. The existing screw-head underside at 9.5 mm and
keeper-washer top at 20.5 mm are unchanged, as are the carrier and guide.

## Evidence boundary

Tests preserve vendor volumes, validate nominal contact/nonpenetration,
check both openings at closed/half/open poses, and reject a reversed upper
bush. Nominal geometry does not prove clearance tolerances, bearing preload,
axle strength, PETG creep/wear or independent retention. The 10 mm spacer,
extra spacing ring, screw, washers and nut remain parametric candidates;
no spacer STEP was supplied in this import.
