# CFG.30/30 SH-6-C33 vendor geometry

Source: Elesa+Ganter / CADENAS. `CFG3030.stp` is an unchanged copy of the
repository-supplied `Hinges CFG.30_30 SH-6-C33 (0).stp`. Its embedded STEP
header identifies CADENAS and CC BY-ND 4.0; retain that attribution and file.
Manufacturer drawing: https://www.elesa-ganter.com/siteassets/PDF/EN/CFG..pdf

The source contains three solids: positive-X leaf, nickel-plated steel pin,
negative-X leaf. Pin axis is source Z at X=Y=0; mounting plane is Y=-8 mm.
Overall closed dimensions are 54 × 16 × 36 mm; fixing centres X=±17.5 mm.
The loader preserves the solids without scaling, cutting or rebuilding them.
Rigid placement/animation is applied separately to each assembly component.

Three complete hinges join frame to primary leaf and three join the two
bifold leaves, on each opening: 12 complete purchased hinges including pins.
The two PA components each account for half a purchased assembly; the pin
is included (zero additional purchase quantity), not omitted physically.
Frame mounting uses a rigid 180° X rotation, putting the mounting face at
Y=+8; interleaf mounting retains Y=-8. Both sides share the native pin axis.

Real geometry is not a load or installation approval. M6 countersunk screws,
slot inserts, engagement, joint stiffness and practical clearance still need
confirmation. The manufacturer's nominal angular range is -100° to +180°
from coplanar mounting faces; actual assembly interference is checked separately.
