## Carrier and closing tabs

The carrier is one native metal solid, cut from an 80×40×6 angle to 18 mm
width. Two Ø6.5 root holes remain at H−83 and H−113; the Ø4.5 axle hole stays
at the existing roller datum, and the shelf top remains H−44.1.
For stock preparation: both root holes are at mid-cut-width (9 mm),
38.9 and 68.9 mm below the outer shelf top. The axle hole is at mid-cut-width
and 21 mm from the outside back face of the long leg (15 mm from its inside
mounting face). The 80 mm
leg ends at H−116.1. Its inside vertical face contacts the actual exterior
free-stile face. The initial isolated screen preserved the outside face and
left a 2 mm mounting gap; integration corrects that by moving the stock angle
2 mm toward the leaf. The axle remains fixed, so its hole position in the
stock flange is adjusted accordingly. The R7 root and actual metal screw/
washer envelopes are included. Catalogue gross stock mass is 97.38 g per
slice before holes; partial door load screening uses this rather than a box.

Each root proposes an M6×14 cap screw and 1.6 mm washer through the 6 mm leg.
The 14 mm under-head length leaves 6.4 mm beyond the mounting face, before
accounting for nut setback. This is not a verified engagement length. Exact
BPN nut seating, usable threads, bolt grade, torque, locking, hole bearing,
angle bending and guide reaction remain unvalidated.

Four closing tabs retain the current 60×50×4 geometry and two Ø6.5 holes on
30 mm vertical pitch, using 50×4 flat stock cut to 60 mm. In the 60×50 outline,
hole centres are 15 mm from one cut end and 10 / 40 mm across the stock width. The thinner Motedis
3 mm plate was not selected. Existing two-M6 mounting schedule and contact
pad/impact uncertainties remain. Deburr every edge and protect exposed steel.

## Bolted rail ends and keeper preparation

Each of four keeper strips is 20×4, widened outward so the 10 mm axle slot
and ±15 mm fixing rows stay unchanged. Ends are 4.5 mm inside the old rail-end
datums. Cut length is rail length minus 9 mm: defaults 730.5 mm left / 661 mm
rear, two each. Drill fifteen Ø4.5 holes per strip at the existing header
stations (7.5 mm from each finished end to the nearest hole). Each bottom end has a 5 mm-long, full 4 mm-thickness bevel, achievable
by a saw/file operation. This accommodates the rolled end-angle root instead
of pretending stock angle has a sharp inside corner. Check actual seating
before tightening; do not draw an ill-fitting joint together using the bolts.

Each end barrier is a 50 mm slice of 30×20×4 angle, with its 20 mm foot beneath
the keeper strips and its 30 mm upright stopping roller overtravel. Saw a
10 mm-wide notch from the foot's free edge back to the heel, clearing the
axle path and local fillet while keeping the upright. Drill two Ø4.5 holes
at 12 mm from the outside end and ±15 mm across the width. Countersink the
underside 90° to Ø8 for the specified flush-head screws. The upright's inner
face preserves the former end-bar datum; its top sits 3 mm below the header.

Eight end positions use nominal M4×40 countersunk screws, whose overall
length includes the head. All eight are actual model solids with conical
seating and full shaft envelopes; threads/recesses are not vendor CAD. The
head face is flush with the angle underside, removing the investigation's
0.1 mm head-to-carrier vertical margin: the nominal vertical gap is now
3.1 mm. A larger ISO head or proud screw must not be silently substituted.
The countersink leaves 2.25 mm nominal cylindrical hole land through the
4 mm foot. Its bearing/pull-through capacity is unvalidated.

The end clamp stack is 4 mm angle + 4 mm keeper + 23 mm metal sleeve + 2 mm
bridge washer = 33 mm. M4×40 therefore projects 7 mm beyond the header face,
not 7 mm of guaranteed thread engagement. Actual header slot nuts remain
unmodeled. The other 52 positions retain M4×35 schedule entries and their
29 mm metal stack. No end screw is duplicated in the module fixing schedule.

Both mounting rows connect each angle to the header through the existing
metal sleeve/bridge-washer stack. The PETG body is not the primary bolt-clamp
load path. Sleeves and bridge washers remain cut/selected metal items with
unconfirmed final supply and tolerance stack. Keeper end bevels, countersinks,
bolt spacing and preloads need load/impact acceptance; replacing welds does
not itself establish a rated backup restraint.

## Assembly and validation boundaries

Support the leaf independently. Fit guide bodies, metal sleeves and bridge
washers; insert roller/bush/washer components with the lower carrier stack
open. Offer the separate keeper bars and end angles from below, align the
10 mm slot and lightly fasten. Confirm bevel/root seating and flush screw
heads, then tighten using the approved torque once available. Complete the
carrier and lower axle stack, square the leaves and adjust the closed catches.
Removing the retainer or disconnecting the carrier requires independent door
support. Nominal driver routes use 80 mm straight shafts; actual hand/tool,
thread, seating and tolerance checks remain distinct from motion clearance.

The existing rigid model and full collision policies remain in force. No
collision exclusions were added. Flexible brush intersections remain visible
as unknowns; the actual brush holder still needs sourcing and installation
validation. Catalogue root radii do not prove supplier tolerances. Nominal
clearance, mounting contact, dropped-washer capture and end-bar interception
are geometric evidence, not structural, tilt/drop, slam or fatigue approval.

Outstanding: small-quantity certified stock and preparation quotes; actual
angle/fastener/slot-nut sections and tolerances; final preload/locking and
assembly tools; brush mounting; applied guide, racking and backup impact
loads; connection/washer/sleeve strength; physical fit, corrosion treatment,
wear and commissioning. The four gentle PETG 88° travel stops and two closed
GN4470 kits remain; parked catches remain deliberately absent.
