Draft only; not sent. All dimensions in mm. Request a technical response and
quotation for delivery to Lisbon, Portugal, with a separate price for one
rear-opening prototype and the complete two-opening assembly.

We are assembling a CNC enclosure with supplier-prepared Wolweiss 3030
aluminium door frames and 4 mm inset polycarbonate. The user will bolt and
adjust prepared parts, with no on-site metal cutting, drilling or fabrication.
The folding doors are supported by hinges; the top guide provides horizontal
guidance only. Please review the attached revision-b.svg and revision-b.json.
They are preliminary design studies and are not manufacturing drawings.

The rear nominal opening is 750 wide, with 347.5 / 387.5 wide leaves. The
left opening is 824.5 wide, with 384.75 / 424.75 wide leaves. Provisional leaf
height is 702. All jamb and meeting gaps are 5. Hinges are at 120, 351 and
582 above the leaf bottom at each hinge line. The right parking jamb of the
rear opening stays fixed; reducing its old 837 width reallocates 87 to the
fixed-panel region. Include the revised boundary post and panel preparation
in the structural quotation; 87 is not a panel cut width.

The current design study uses an 88° primary parked angle and approximately
175.6° relative interleaf fold. Please confirm the CFG orientation allows
this with the actual profile, screws, inserts and hinge bodies. The selected
8 mm pivot-to-mounting-plane offset is from existing supplier STEP geometry.
Confirm permissible combined loading for three frame hinges carrying the
whole pair and three interleaf hinges carrying the secondary leaf. Use a
preliminary 4 kg allowance per leaf; return actual component masses and
joint-rigidity/fastener requirements. No capacity is inferred by multiplying
one hinge's rating by three.

The top guide axle is vertical and lies over the secondary free stile,
15 from its free edge, on the closed door midplane. Preliminary rail section:
23 clear internal width, 24 clear internal height, 3 walls and bottom steel
keeper strips leaving a 10 slot. Roller diameter is 22. A separate 20 diameter
steel keeper must retain the axle if the POM wheel fails. Provide vertical
float without loading the roller axially, positive end retention, a locked
metal axle and an independently acting metal parked stop. Please return a
dimensioned axle/spacer/bearing/keeper stack and rail section or a documented
standard alternative. These custom part identifiers are project references.

Do not locate the far rail end stop at the closed roller position: the rear
guide centre moves from x=730 to a maximum x=733.33 as opening starts, then
returns toward the parked x=99.73. The left range is x=102.50–807.52, with
closure at x=804.5. These coordinates are from the fixed opening boundary
at the parking end. Add roller radius, carrier-stop geometry, end plates and
assembly clearances when determining rail cuts.

Support each rail along a structural header tied to posts at both ends.
Confirm the rail/bracket/connection response under the actual guide reactions
through travel; the initial 100 N transverse guide-force screen is not a
validated operating-load specification. Include cantilever bending, torsion,
slot slip, stops, fastener pull-out and the existing enclosure loads. The
initial total lateral guide movement target is 0.5. Request guide-line
adjustment ±3 and height adjustment ±2 in the prepared mounting arrangement.

Available outward workshop space is 450 behind the rear opening and 800
outside the left opening. Aim for a complete rear hardware sweep no greater
than 400. The sampled leaf study, with a provisional 15 fittings allowance,
gives 372.8 rear / 410.0 left and parked usable widths 619.6 / 691.4.
Please replace that allowance with actual hardware geometry and tolerances.
Keep handles and catches outside the interleaf pinch/folding space.

Please specify a panel liner compatible with 4 mm polycarbonate in the
8.2 mm profile slot, allowing edge engagement and thermal movement, and
provide prepared panel cuts. For door dust seals, assess Mink STL 2108-K21
(8 mm slot family, 10 mm PBT bristles) or a documented compatible alternative.
Quote a sample before full lengths. Provide a removable header dust cover,
light wiping contact and cleaning access without obstructing guide travel.

Return complete preparation drawings, component references, screw lengths,
nut geometry, assembly/torque instructions, catch retention forces, materials,
finishes, dimensional tolerances, lead times, VAT and freight. Identify any
required design changes explicitly. We will review the drawings and test one
prepared opening before releasing the complete assembly for manufacture.
